# Remote Work Policy

Office-based staff at Meridian Freight Cooperative may work remotely under the hybrid arrangement described here. Warehouse, dispatch floor and driver roles are not eligible because their work is location-bound.

## Remote days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote days are agreed with the manager at the start of each month and recorded in Timeboard. Tuesdays are anchor days: every eligible employee is expected in the office on Tuesday unless on approved leave.

## Core hours

Regardless of location, employees must be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 Eastern Time. Meetings that need the whole team are scheduled inside core hours.

## Equipment

Each remote-eligible employee receives a home office stipend of $400 per calendar year for a chair, desk, monitor or similar equipment. The stipend is claimed through Ledgerly with receipts and does not roll over.

## Connectivity and security

Remote access to internal systems goes through the Bastion VPN. Work must be done on cooperative-issued laptops. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN connected. Printing confidential documents at home is prohibited.

## Working abroad

Working from outside the United States for more than 10 consecutive days requires prior approval from People Operations because of tax and insurance rules.
