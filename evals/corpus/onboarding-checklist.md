# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

## Before day one

- People Operations sends the welcome pack and the first-week schedule.
- IT ships the laptop so it arrives before the start date; remote hires receive it at home.
- The manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the same team.

## Day one

- Laptop set-up, Sentinel Auth enrolment and KeyVault account.
- Safety induction for hub-based roles.
- Introduction meeting with the buddy and the team.

## First 90 days

Every new employee works through a 30-60-90 day plan agreed with their manager during the first week. The plan lists learning goals, first deliverables and the people to meet. Progress is discussed at the 30-day and 60-day check-ins, and the first formal review takes place at 90 days.

## Learning

Required courses are assigned in Compass, the learning portal: code of conduct, information security awareness, and role-specific modules. Courses must be completed within the first 30 days.

## Buddy programme

The buddy answers day-to-day questions, joins the new employee for lunch in the first week and checks in weekly for the first 2 months. Buddies receive a $75 meal allowance for the programme.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. Confirmation of employment follows a satisfactory 90-day review and a short check-in at 6 months.
