# Performance Review Cycle

## Timing

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. The April review is a development conversation; the October review includes ratings, compensation decisions and promotions.

## Ratings

Employees receive a rating on a 1 to 5 scale, where 3 means fully meeting expectations. Ratings are proposed by the manager and confirmed by a calibration committee for each department so that ratings are consistent across teams.

## Process

1. The employee writes a self-assessment in Summit, the performance tool, by the end of the first week of the review month.
2. Peers nominated by the employee and the manager provide feedback in Summit.
3. The manager drafts the review and proposed rating.
4. The calibration committee meets in the third week of the month.
5. The manager holds the review conversation in the last week of the month.

## Promotions

Promotion nominations are submitted in the October cycle only. A promotion case includes the last two reviews, examples of work at the next level and a sponsor from outside the employee's direct reporting line.

## Compensation

Merit increases decided in October take effect on 1 January. Employees who joined after 1 July are included in the following year's October cycle.

## Disagreements

An employee who disagrees with a rating may request a review by the department director within 10 business days of the conversation.
