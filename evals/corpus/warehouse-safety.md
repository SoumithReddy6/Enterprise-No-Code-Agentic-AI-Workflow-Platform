# Warehouse Safety Standards

These standards apply at every Meridian Freight Cooperative hub and cross-dock facility.

## Personal protective equipment

High-visibility vests and steel-toe boots are mandatory on the warehouse floor at all times. Hearing protection is required in the sortation area when conveyors are running.

## Forklift operation

Only certified operators may drive forklifts and reach trucks. Certification is renewed every 24 months through the hub training coordinator. Operators complete a pre-shift inspection checklist before the first use of each shift. Pedestrians have right of way; operators sound the horn at every aisle crossing.

## Racking and stacking

The maximum stack height for palletised freight is 4.2 metres. Damaged racking is tagged out immediately and reported to the facilities team. Load capacities are posted on every rack upright and must not be exceeded.

## Incidents and near misses

All injuries and near misses are reported within the same shift using the SafeReport app. The hub safety officer reviews every report within 2 business days. The regional safety programme is led by Priya Natarajan, Director of Safety.

## Training

New hub employees complete safety induction on their first day and a refresher every 12 months. Refresher completion is tracked in Compass.
