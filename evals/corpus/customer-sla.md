# Customer Service Level Agreement

This document summarises the delivery commitments Meridian Freight Cooperative makes to contracted customers.

## Delivery windows

- **Standard service**: delivery within 3 business days of pickup for domestic shipments.
- **Express service**: delivery within 24 hours of pickup between hubs in the express network.
- **Scheduled service**: delivery within the 2-hour window agreed at booking.

## Service credits

If a shipment misses its committed window, the customer receives a service credit of 15 percent of the shipment charge. Credits are applied automatically to the next invoice. Weather events declared by the National Weather Service and customs holds are excluded from credits.

## Claims

Loss or damage claims are filed in ClaimDesk within 14 days of the delivery date or expected delivery date. Claims are acknowledged within 2 business days and resolved within 30 days. Liability is limited to the declared value of the shipment, up to $100 per kilogram unless additional insurance was purchased.

## Tracking and notifications

Customers receive tracking updates at pickup, each hub scan, out-for-delivery and delivery. Proof of delivery, including signature and photo, is available in the customer portal within 1 hour of delivery.

## Support

The customer care line is staffed from 06:00 to 22:00 Eastern, seven days a week.
