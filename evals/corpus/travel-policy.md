# Business Travel Policy

## Booking

All flights, hotels and rental cars are booked through Voyager, the cooperative's travel platform. Bookings made outside Voyager are reimbursed only with director approval. Trips must be booked at least 14 days in advance when the dates are known.

## Flights

Economy class is the standard for flights under 6 hours. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy or business class. The cooperative's preferred carriers are the members of the Skyline Alliance; other carriers may be chosen when they are at least $150 cheaper for the same itinerary.

## Hotels

Hotel rooms are capped at $220 per night before taxes. Cities with a higher published cap in Voyager, such as New York and San Francisco, use that cap instead. Room upgrades, minibar and in-room entertainment are personal expenses.

## Ground transport

Rental cars are limited to the intermediate class. Ride-hailing and taxis are reimbursed with receipts. Personal vehicle use follows the mileage rate in the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## Meals

Meals during travel are covered by the per diem in the Expense Reimbursement Policy and are not claimed separately.

## Safety

Travellers register international trips in Voyager so the Security Operations Centre can reach them in an emergency. Travel to countries under a Level 3 or Level 4 government advisory requires approval from the Chief Operating Officer.
