# Dispatch and Route Scheduling

## Route planning

Routes for the next day are generated in the Waypoint routing engine and finalised by 18:00 the previous day. Dispatchers review Waypoint's suggested routes for hub constraints, driver qualifications and customer time windows before release. Drivers see their finalised route in the RoadPulse driver app.

## Driving hours

Drivers may drive a maximum of 11 hours within a 14-hour duty window and must take a 30-minute break after 8 hours of driving. Waypoint blocks any route that would exceed these limits. Dispatchers cannot override a driving-hours block.

## Hub codes

Every hub has a code made from its airport-style city abbreviation and a number, for example ATL-1 for the Atlanta main hub, DEN-2 for the Denver cross-dock and PHL-1 for Philadelphia. Hub codes appear on labels, manifests and in Waypoint.

## Changes after release

Same-day changes, such as a pickup added after release, are entered by the dispatch desk and pushed to the driver's app. Drivers acknowledge changes before proceeding. Changes that add more than 45 minutes to a route require dispatcher approval and a check of remaining driving hours.

## Handover

The dispatch desk runs two shifts, 05:00 to 14:00 and 13:30 to 22:30 Eastern. The overlapping half hour is used for a verbal handover of open exceptions.
