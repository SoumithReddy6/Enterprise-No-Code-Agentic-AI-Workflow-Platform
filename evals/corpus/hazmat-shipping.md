# Hazardous Materials Shipping Procedure

Hazardous materials (hazmat) move only through hubs certified for the relevant class and only with drivers holding the correct endorsement.

## Accepted classes

Meridian Freight Cooperative accepts Class 3 flammable liquids, Class 8 corrosives and Class 9 miscellaneous dangerous goods. Explosives, radioactive materials and infectious substances are not accepted.

## Documentation

Every hazmat shipment requires a completed HZ-7 Dangerous Goods Declaration signed by the shipper. The dispatcher verifies the HZ-7 against the shipment before releasing the route and signs the dispatcher approval box. Shipments without a signed HZ-7 are refused at pickup.

## Drivers

Drivers carrying hazmat hold the H endorsement on their commercial licence and complete hazmat refresher training every 3 years. The driver keeps the HZ-7 and the emergency response guide in the cab during transit.

## Placards and packaging

Vehicles carrying placardable quantities display the class placard on all four sides. Packages carry UN-specification packaging marks and orientation arrows where required.

## Segregation

Class 3 flammables are never loaded in the same compartment as Class 8 corrosives. The segregation table posted at each certified hub lists every permitted and prohibited combination.

## Spills

Spills are reported to the dispatch desk and the hub safety officer immediately. Drivers use the spill kit only for spills under 4 litres and otherwise wait for the hazmat response contractor.
