# Procurement and Purchase Approvals

## Approval thresholds

| Purchase amount | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Under $2,000 | Line manager |
| $2,000 to $25,000 | Department director |
| Above $25,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Any contract longer than 12 months | CFO and Legal |

Purchases must not be split into smaller orders to avoid a threshold.

## Quotes

Purchases above $10,000 require 3 competitive quotes attached to the purchase request. Sole-source purchases above $10,000 require a written justification approved by the director.

## Vendor onboarding

New vendors are onboarded in Supplyline before the first purchase order. Onboarding collects tax forms, banking details verified by a call-back to a known phone number, insurance certificates and a sanctions screening result. Vendor banking changes always require the same call-back verification.

## Purchase orders

Every purchase above $500 needs a purchase order issued from Supplyline before the goods or services are ordered. Invoices without a matching purchase order are returned to the vendor.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are for travel and incidental purchases under $500. Card transactions are reconciled in Ledgerly within 30 days.
