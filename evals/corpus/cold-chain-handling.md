# Cold Chain Handling Procedure

Temperature-controlled freight includes pharmaceuticals, vaccines, fresh food and certain chemicals. Breaking the cold chain can make a shipment worthless or dangerous.

## Temperature ranges

- Refrigerated: 2 °C to 8 °C.
- Frozen: −25 °C to −15 °C.
- Controlled room temperature: 15 °C to 25 °C.

The required range is printed on the label and recorded in the manifest.

## Monitoring

Every temperature-controlled shipment travels with a FrostTrack data logger. FrostTrack reports temperature every 5 minutes and raises an alarm to the dispatch desk if the shipment is outside its range for more than 15 minutes. Alarms are acknowledged within 10 minutes and the driver is contacted for corrective action.

## Excursions

Any excursion outside the range for more than 15 minutes is recorded as a temperature excursion. The customer is notified before delivery and decides whether to accept the shipment. Excursion reports are attached to the shipment record and retained with it.

## Pharmaceutical loads

Pharmaceutical and vaccine loads are double-checked at loading: two employees confirm the logger is running, the reefer set point matches the manifest and the doors seal correctly. Both employees sign the loading sheet.

## Equipment

Reefer units are pre-cooled for at least 30 minutes before loading. Loggers are calibrated annually by the supplier.
