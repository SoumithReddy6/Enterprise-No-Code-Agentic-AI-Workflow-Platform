# Fleet Maintenance Standard

Meridian Freight Cooperative operates its own fleet of tractors, box trucks and vans. Preventive maintenance keeps vehicles safe and avoids roadside breakdowns.

## Service intervals

- Engine oil and filter: every 15,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first.
- Full safety inspection: every 90 days, performed at a cooperative maintenance bay.
- Brake inspection: every 30,000 km.
- Annual roadworthiness certification: once per year before the registration renewal date.

## Tyres

Tyres are replaced when tread depth falls below 4 mm on steer axles or 3 mm on drive and trailer axles. Tyre pressure is checked at every fuel stop.

## Telematics

Every vehicle carries a RoadPulse telematics unit that reports location, engine faults, harsh braking and idle time. RoadPulse fault codes create a maintenance ticket automatically. Drivers must not disconnect the RoadPulse unit.

## Daily checks

Drivers complete a pre-trip inspection in the RoadPulse driver app before leaving the yard. Any defect marked "unsafe" grounds the vehicle until a technician clears it.

## Breakdowns

Roadside breakdowns are reported to the dispatch desk, which arranges recovery through the contracted roadside partner. Drivers wait in a safe location and never attempt roadside repairs beyond changing a bulb.
