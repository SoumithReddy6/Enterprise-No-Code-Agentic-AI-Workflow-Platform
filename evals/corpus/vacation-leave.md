# Vacation and Leave

## Annual vacation

Full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly from the start date. Part-time employees accrue pro rata.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried into the next calendar year. Days above the carryover limit are forfeited on 31 December unless a director approves an exception in writing because business needs prevented the employee from taking leave.

## Requesting leave

Vacation is requested in Timeboard at least 2 weeks in advance. Requests of 10 or more consecutive working days need 6 weeks' notice so dispatch and hub coverage can be arranged. Managers respond to requests within 3 business days.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Absences longer than 3 consecutive days require a medical certificate.

## Sabbatical

After 7 years of continuous service an employee may take a paid sabbatical of 4 weeks. Sabbaticals are scheduled with People Operations at least 3 months ahead and cannot be combined with more than 5 vacation days.

## Bereavement and jury duty

Up to 5 paid days are granted for bereavement of an immediate family member. Jury duty is paid for its full duration on presentation of the summons.
