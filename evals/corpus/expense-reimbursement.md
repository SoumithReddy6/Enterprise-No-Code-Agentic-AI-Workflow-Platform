# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Meridian Freight Cooperative reimburses reasonable business expenses that are submitted correctly and on time.

## Submission

All expense reports are filed in Ledgerly, the cooperative's expense system. Reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Reports submitted after 30 days require written approval from the employee's director and are paid at the discretion of Finance.

## Receipts

Itemised receipts are required for any single expense of $25 or more. Credit card statements are not accepted as receipts. Missing receipts must be explained on the Missing Receipt Declaration form inside Ledgerly.

## Meals and per diem

Employees travelling overnight receive a meal per diem of $65 per day. The per diem covers breakfast, lunch, dinner and incidental tips. On the first and last day of travel the per diem is reduced to 75 percent. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at 67 cents per mile, measured from the employee's normal work location. Commuting miles between home and the normal work location are not reimbursable. Tolls and parking are reimbursed at cost with receipts.

## Payment

Approved reports are paid with the next payroll run, typically within 10 business days of manager approval. Questions go to the Finance Help Desk.
