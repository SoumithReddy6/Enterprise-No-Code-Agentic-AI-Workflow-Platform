# Paid Holidays

Meridian Freight Cooperative observes 11 paid holidays each year plus 1 floating holiday that each employee schedules with their manager.

## Observed holidays

- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day

Offices are closed from 24 December to 26 December inclusive. When a holiday falls on a weekend it is observed on the nearest weekday.

## Floating holiday

The floating holiday may be used for any personal or religious observance. It must be taken within the calendar year and is requested in Timeboard like vacation.

## Operations on holidays

Hubs and the dispatch desk keep a reduced schedule on holidays because express shipments still move. Employees who work a holiday receive holiday pay at 1.5 times their base rate plus a substitute day off within 60 days.

## Regional differences

Employees based in Canada follow the Canadian statutory holiday calendar published separately by People Operations.
