# Account Security Standard

## Passwords

Passwords for cooperative systems must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any of the previous 8 passwords. Passwords are rotated every 180 days; the identity system prompts 14 days before expiry. Passwords must never be shared, written on paper or stored in browser autofill.

## Password manager

Employees store credentials in KeyVault, the approved password manager. Shared team credentials, such as carrier portal logins, live in KeyVault shared folders with access reviewed quarterly by the team lead.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication through Sentinel Auth is mandatory for email, the VPN, Ledgerly, Timeboard and every administrative console. The Sentinel Auth mobile app is the preferred factor; SMS codes are allowed only as a temporary fallback for 7 days after a phone change.

## Lost or compromised devices

Report a lost laptop or phone to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour of noticing the loss. The device is remotely wiped and all sessions are revoked.

## Phishing

Suspicious messages are reported with the Report Phish button in the mail client. Employees who click a simulated phishing link complete a 20-minute refresher in Compass.
